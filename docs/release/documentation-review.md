6. **Paid pilot before paid infrastructure.** The roadmap and lifecycle register require identity, verified billing, refunds/support, privacy/consent and measurement gates before payment or renewal testing. A non-commercial usability study is a separate approved activity, not a simulated paid-pilot result.
