# Funnel map v0

Updated October 28, 2026. **Confidence: `inferred`.** Derived from [STRATEGY.md](../../STRATEGY.md), [go-to-market](../commercial/go-to-market.md) and the [market research](../research/market/commercial-strategy.md), not from observed users. M1 replaces it with v1 once the evidence ledger and interviews exist; until then every row is a hypothesis to test, not a fact to market.

Steps: **Sees → Tries → Captures → First useful week → Second useful week → Pays → Renews.** Activation is the first useful week; the north-star is the second and later ones.

| Move | Belief needed (their words, to be confirmed) | Trigger moment | Main blocker; can product fix it? | Observable signal (future event) | Observable today | Expectation to test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sees → Tries | "It works with recipes I already saved, not a new catalog" | Saving a creator recipe; planning the week; grocery day | "Yet another recipe app"; trust in import quality. Partly: honest demos | Install or first open by source | Nothing | Task demos (share → review → week) beat feature lists |
| Tries → Captures | "Fixing an import takes less time than retyping it" | First shared or pasted recipe | Extraction errors, missing yield or macros. Yes: review UX, fidelity (M2) | Import attempt / review completed / abandoned | Nothing | Correction minutes per import predict drop-off |
| Captures → First useful week | "It plans around the time I actually have" | Sunday or pre-shop planning | Unknown recipe times (prep known for 5 of 119 seed recipes), planner fatigue. Yes: time capture, small proposals (M3) | Week confirmed + shop progress + cooking completed | Nothing | Users who cook once in week 1 return for week 2 |
| First → Second useful week | "Week two is easier: ready food and the list carry over" | Leftovers; a disrupted week | Recovery cost when plans change. Yes: local swaps, reuse | Second confirmed week with ready-food reuse | Nothing | Ready-food reuse raises second-week rate |
| Second week → Pays | "Goal-fit planning saves me real time each week" | Asking for a target-fit week; rebalancing after a missed cook | Value unproven; cheaper alternatives (Paprika one-time, Plan to Eat). Partly | Upgrade view / trial / purchase | Nothing | Upsell at a goal-fit request converts better than at import limits |
| Pays → Renews | "It stayed right: numbers, list and history" | Renewal date | Wrong numbers, lost data, broken trust. Yes: provenance, reliability | Renewal / cancel with reason | Nothing | Cancellation reasons cluster on trust, not price |

## What cannot be observed yet

Mealbook has no analytics, accounts or public release, so no step is measurable from product data. Until the M6 pilot, the only sources are the owner's dogfood log (EV-08, n = 1), moderated sessions (EV-10) and public evidence (EV-09). Events above are names to design, not events that exist.
