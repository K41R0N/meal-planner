# Roadmap milestones on GitHub

Updated October 28, 2026. The roadmap is [the commercial roadmap](../roadmap/commercial-release.md). Each GitHub milestone mirrors one of its stages, with the same name and the same gate. This file only maps them: which evals measure each gate and how stages are sequenced. It adds no stages, gates or stop rules of its own. When the roadmap changes, change it first, then this file and [`.github/pm/board.json`](../../.github/pm/board.json).

**Methods are not milestones.** The [operating loops](README.md), [evals register](../evals/README.md) and [research and growth methods](../growth/README.md) are how stages get executed and checked. They appear inside a stage's issues only where the roadmap itself calls for research or measurement: Stage 2's supervised pilot, Stage 4's recruitment, interviews and instrumentation, and Stage 5's store copy and growth measurement.

Thresholds the roadmap leaves open, such as held-out sample sizes or fidelity targets, are set in that stage's plan before any scored run.

| Milestone | Roadmap stage | Gate (from the roadmap, shortened) | Measured by | Sequencing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S0 | 0 — Preserve the working personal foundation | Document the foundation and verify clean build and backup behavior. No new commercial claims, checkout or public creator-catalog distribution. | CI build and EV-01; EV-03 including a backup round trip | Current |
| S1 | 1 — Make recipes and nutrition trustworthy | A representative held-out set meets predeclared source-fidelity, ingredient/unit, yield and serving-basis thresholds, with denominators and abstention reported. The research seed does not ship as a licensed starter set without rights review. | EV-02, EV-04 | After S0 |
| S2 | 2 — Prove the repeatable week and numeric-goal engine | Held-out scenarios cover feasible and impossible goals, partial-day disclosure, unknown macros/yield, locked recipes, edited composition, leftovers, changed cooking windows, allergy exclusions and purchase invariants. A supervised pilot shows users can reach a usable second week. | EV-05, EV-10 | After S1 |
| S3 | 3 — Add accounts, Android, sync and authoritative entitlements | Android physical-device and interrupted-network tests, source/backup migration and restore, no cross-user leakage, purchase/expiry/refund webhooks and support exercises pass in staging. Production owner, privacy documents, publisher accounts and budget limits exist before paid users. | Defined in the stage plan | After S2. Decisions O002 and O003 come first ([decision registry](../decisions/README.md)) |
| S3A | 3A — Agent-friendly shared workspace and MCP | An authorized source → reviewed recipe → confirmed week → shopping scenario is visible in the real app and tested clients; isolation, revocation, stale approvals, retries, concurrent stock, unknown evidence and interruption are tested; quota and job state and measured cost survive loss; core access works without inference or Pro; no "most agent-friendly" claim without a comparison test. | EV-06; the agent plan's user-outcome gate | Shared commands and the local bridge (plan units U1–U2) alongside S1–S2; account-backed remote work after S3 |
| S4 | 4 — Closed commercial pilot before broad marketing | Production readiness register signed off; support can recover billing and data incidents; actual payment and renewal evidence informs whether to scale. Decision rules: 20 usable weeks, 12 second weeks, 8 payments, 5 first renewals of 30. | EV-11 | After S3 |
| S5 | 5 — Release and growth, only after the gates | Android and responsive web with accurate store descriptions, honest import limits, plan terms, support, export and deletion, compliant attribution, incidents and rollback. | Launch measurement (see [growth methods](../growth/README.md)) | After S4 |

The roadmap's own stop rule for Stage 4 applies as written: if people import but do not cook a second week, improve the loop rather than adding more AI marketing. Stage 5's out-of-scope list also stands: no store listing, payments, website publication, inference host, public paid creator seed or outreach under the current approval.
