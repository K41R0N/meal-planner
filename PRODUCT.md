# Mealbook product context

Approved 2026-10-26: implement the Today-first experience architecture and Atlantic/oyster/citrus card-led design, without publishing. `K41R0N/meal-planner` remains the central source; Storyteller Tactics informs cards and interaction patterns on web and Android, never its paid content.

The user's hardest jobs are finding time to cook and turning useful recipes into groceries that cover more meals. The app opens Today's recipe with a state-matched next action, not a planner form. Four primary destinations are Today, My week, Recipe book and Shop. History, goals, importing, settings and advanced organization are secondary.

Core loop: discover/import → review source and uncertainty → save → optionally assign useful batch portions and real cooking windows → shop remaining compatible ingredients → cook/resume → confirm actual produced/retained portions → explicitly record what was eaten → reuse ready food or plan a replacement. Planning is not cooking; cooking is not eating; prepared-food snapshots do not change when book recipes are edited.

Today stays stable across refresh and cooking/eating. No-week users can cook or shop one recipe. Cooking saves steps, ingredient checks and timers. Completion records actual yield and user-supplied storage/reheat notes without logging intake, and can record how long the cook took: when the person keeps that time, later weeks can fit that recipe, at that same amount, into a cooking window. Actual consumption records nutrition snapshots and reduces available food; Undo restores both. Frozen food is not silently treated as defrosted or safe to eat.

My week starts with useful portions and actual cooking opportunities. Randomization, saved filters, favorites, distinct recipe counts and ingredient overlap are controlled helpers, not compulsory calendar setup. Unknown timing is not zero or a verified fit. Preserve locked/cooked/in-progress choices, allow explicit meal/batch swaps with grocery impact, and preserve extras and compatible cross-week ready portions. Unknown source yield and unconfirmed personal portions stay visibly uncertain.

Shop groups compatible ingredient quantities by aisle and shows remaining needs, partial already-have coverage, purchase history and recipe contributions. Completed preparation does not ask for the same raw procurement again; shopping history is not an inventory claim or erased by eating. Never invent cup-to-gram conversions, prices, package leftovers or savings. Optional purchase/preparation matching is user confirmed and retains original food variants/prep instructions.

YouTube import is an online, description-first/captions-if-available review flow with pasted creator text as a fallback. Save continues to Cook today, Add to my week or Keep in my book with the recipe selected. Do not download video/audio or estimate nutrition from ingredient guesses. Source-reported macros, their basis and unknown fields remain distinct. Users control tags, categories, meal roles, suitability and all macro/ingredient filters.

Private on-device state, compatible version-one backups and the 119-entry foundation are retained. No accounts, organization switcher, cloud sync or server-side personal-data database. Core flows and bundled recipes work offline after download/cache activation. Newly imported remote imagery and further online extraction require a connection.

Native React Native/Expo packaging, multi-user support, cross-device sync, public release and a hosted Ollaya/Laya organization service are later work. Core use cannot require optional inference. Installed development plugins guide the work; runnable audits and native account limitations must be reported honestly, not presented as awards or usability studies.

## Approved agent-friendly direction — not yet implemented

Approved October 27, 2026: Mealbook should expose a correctness-first data/action service to users' own MCP-capable assistants. The assistant supplies reasoning; Mealbook supplies source evidence, validation, exact quantities, owned records and controlled transitions. No embedded conversational agent, inference-key collection or provider billing is required for this path. Existing managed extraction remains unchanged until a separately implemented source-fetch-plus-validation path exists.

The ordinary Today/week/book/shop cards remain the human workspace. Future web/Android and MCP clients share one account-scoped application API; remote tools cannot read the current private browser database by themselves. Reads/previews require authorized scopes; reversible writes require specific approval or narrow standing permission; actual cooking/eating and destructive actions require explicit confirmation. See [agent architecture](docs/architecture/agents/README.md), [tool contract](docs/reference/agents/tool-contract.md) and [client eligibility research](docs/research/agent-integrations/README.md). This approval adds documentation and a future implementation/scaling plan, not a working connector or public endpoint.
